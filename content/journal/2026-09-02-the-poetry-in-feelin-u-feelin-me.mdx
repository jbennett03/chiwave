It’s not a secret that The Diary of Alicia Keys serves to immerse you into the feelings of Alicia Keys. Feeling U, Feeling Me to me is a standout track on the back half of the album due to the simplicity in its words. It draws you into her world despite the short listen at 2 minutes and 7 seconds. The lyrics don’t start until almost a minute into the track and it hits IMMEDIATELY. 

Two hour long conversations on the phone 

Can’t get you outta my mind

Baby, are you feelin’ me, feelin’ you?

She puts feelings into the words that describe the beauty of the start of a new relationship. The endless words you have for each other, the butterflies in your stomach, the way your heart skips a beat. All of it is present in just these words. A feeling so strong that you as the listener can feel it in your own body just how lost she is in this person. Beauty in simplicity. She ends with

Everything you say and everything you do 

Gets me lost in you days at a time

Tell me are you feelin’ me feelin’ you?




Are you?
